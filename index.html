THE INVISIBLE LAYER OF MODERN LIFE

Your lights work.
That isn’t the whole story.

Modern electronics can introduce harmonics and high-frequency line interference onto wiring. “Dirty electricity” is a broad shorthand, not a diagnosis. Harmonic distortion, power factor and radiated fields describe different electrical phenomena. Knowing the difference matters.

01

Measure the right thing

An EMI Line Monitor helps compare line-interference readings at an outlet. It is not a medical test or a measurement of every type of EMF.

02

Filtration works on the line

Explore plug-in and installed filtration for unwanted electrical line noise. A line filter does not directly block radio-frequency waves.

03

Shielding is a separate job

FieldShield uses an eight-layer grounded construction for targeted shielding applications. Placement, dimensions and grounding matter; it is not interchangeable with line filtration.
